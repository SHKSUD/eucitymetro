Logistics Intelligence

Is the Pantheon wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the Pantheon is highly accessible. The entrance from Piazza della Rotonda is level. The interior floor is famous for being perfectly flat ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration), originally designed for water drainage. There are no steps inside the main temple. Note: Entry now requires a ticket, so book an 'accessible/disability' slot online to skip the long outdoor queue.
Where are the accessible restrooms near the Pantheon?
There are no toilets inside the Pantheon. The best nearby PRM facility is in the 'Biblioteca Casanatense' or the 'Hotel Nazionale' in Piazza di Monte Citorio ($300\text{ meters}$ away), which are both fully accessible via lifts.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Pantheon?
The best drop-off is 'Piazza della Rotonda' (South Side) or 'Via dei Pastini' ($41.899800, 12.476800$). Taxis can get quite close, leaving only a $30\text{-meter}$ roll on flat (15Hz) stones to the entrance.
What is the sensory profile of the Pantheon?
It has a 'celestial and hushed' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is very quiet ($45\text{dB}$) with a unique upward echo due to the Oculous (the hole in the roof). Visually, the light beam is very high-contrast. Physically, it is ultra-stable ($0\text{Hz}$). It is an excellent sensory refuge.
Can I see the tombs (like Raphael's) from a chair?
Yes, all the tombs and altars are located around the perimeter of the single-level floor. There are no barriers or steps to reach any of them.
How do I skip the line with a wheelchair?
Show your disability ID or point to your chair to the staff at the front of the queue. They will direct you to the 'Priority' lane immediately, bypassing the wait.

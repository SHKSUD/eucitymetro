Logistics Intelligence

Are the Capitoline Museums accessible?
Yes, the museums are fully accessible via a complex network of elevators. The 'Galleria Lapidaria' connects the two main buildings underground and is perfectly level ($0\text{Hz}$). The Tabularium terrace, offering the best view of the Roman Forum, is also accessible by lift.
Where are the accessible restrooms at the Capitoline?
The best PRM restrooms are on the ground floor near the ticket office and in the New Wing (Palazzo dei Conservatori). They are high-standard and modern.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Capitoline?
The best drop-off is 'Via del Tempio di Giove' ($41.892700, 12.483200$). This avoids the massive climb and puts you at the rear elevator entrance of the museum.
What is the sensory profile of the Capitoline?
It has a 'solemn and stony' sensory profile. Inside, it is much quieter than the Vatican ($50\text{dB}$). Visually, the massive statues provide high-contrast. Physically, the interior is ultra-stable ($0\text{Hz}$).
Can I see the original Marcus Aurelius statue?
Yes, the bronze statue is located in a modern, level-access glass hall that is 100% step-free.
How do I ask for the hidden elevator?
If the door is closed at the back, ask the guard: 'Posso usare l'ascensore per disabili?'. They are very helpful with the key-operated lifts.

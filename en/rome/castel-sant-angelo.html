Logistics Intelligence

Is Castel Sant'Angelo wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the fortress has been modernized with a large internal lift. The ground floor, the main internal ramp (The Helical Ramp), and the middle tiers are accessible. The very top terrace (where the angel statue is) has some narrow steps and is not fully accessible, but the lower terraces offer 360-degree views and are 100% level ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration).
Where are the accessible restrooms at the Castle?
There are modern PRM restrooms located on the ground floor near the bookshop and on the 'Cafeteria' level (accessible via lift). They are high-standard with $1.8\text{-meter}$ turning radii.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Castel Sant'Angelo?
The best drop-off is 'Lungotevere Castello' ($41.903100, 12.466300$). This allows for a 0Hz roll directly into the main entrance gate, avoiding the historic bridge and its vibrations.
What is the sensory profile of the Castle?
It has a 'stony and echoing' sensory profile. Acoustically, the internal ramps have a unique $70\text{dB}$ echo. Visually, it transitions from dark tunnels to very bright terraces. Physically, it is stable ($5\text{Hz}$). It is much cooler than the Forum, making it a good midday retreat.
Is the internal ramp too steep?
The historic 'Helical Ramp' is long and has a $6\%$ gradient. While it is paved with smooth brick, it can be tiring for manual chairs. It is recommended to use the modern lift for a $0\text{Hz}$ vertical transition.
How do I ask for the elevator?
Ask at the ticket counter: 'Per favore, posso usare l'ascensore?'. A staff member will usually need to activate it with a key and accompany you.

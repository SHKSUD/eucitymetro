Logistics Intelligence

Is Tiergarten wheelchair accessible?
Yes, Tiergarten is one of the most accessible large city parks in the world. The vast majority of paths are wide, flat asphalt ($5\text{Hz}$ vibration). There are no steep hills. It is a perfect 'low-sensory' escape from the city. All major monuments within the park have level-access paths.
Where are the accessible restrooms in Tiergarten?
There are several 'Berliner WC' units (automated public toilets) scattered throughout the park (e.g., near the Rousseau Island and the Victory Column). They are Eurokey-accessible, level, and modern.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Tiergarten?
The best drop-off is 'Straße des 17. Juni / Sowjetisches Ehrenmal' ($52.516400, 13.372100$). This places you in the heart of the park on a wide, level asphalt path.
What is the sensory profile of Tiergarten?
It has a 'verdant and peaceful' sensory profile. It is a major sensory refuge ($45\text{dB}$ to $55\text{dB}$). Physically, it is very stable. Visually, it is dominated by soft greens and dappled light.
Are the bridges in the park accessible?
Most bridges, like the ones near the English Garden, have very gentle gradients ($<4\%$) and are paved with smooth wood or stone, making them easy for manual chairs.
How do I find a quiet spot?
Move away from the 'Straße des 17. Juni' (the main road). The area around the 'Bellevue Palace' and the southern lakes is much quieter and offers 0Hz-vibration asphalt paths.

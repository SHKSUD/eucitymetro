Logistics Intelligence

Is the Berlin TV Tower accessible?
Yes, the TV Tower is wheelchair accessible via a high-speed elevator. However, due to safety regulations, you must be able to leave your wheelchair and use the evacuation chair in an emergency, or bring a companion. It is mandatory to book a special ticket online. The observation deck is 100% level with $0\text{Hz}$ vibration once inside.
Where are the accessible restrooms at Alexanderplatz?
The best PRM restrooms are in the 'Alexa' shopping mall (Level 1) or the 'Galeria Kaufhof'. Both are high-spec and accessible via large internal lifts.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Alexanderplatz?
The best drop-off is 'Panoramastraße 1' ($52.520800, 13.409400$), directly at the foot of the TV Tower. This avoids the crowds of the main square and offers a $0\text{Hz}$ roll to the entrance.
What is the sensory profile of Alexanderplatz?
It has a 'high-stimulus and urban' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is very loud ($80\text{dB}$) with trains and musicians. Visually, it is high-contrast with massive screens. Physically, it is ultra-stable ($5\text{Hz}$).
Can I visit the rotating restaurant?
Yes, the 'Sphere' restaurant is fully accessible. There is a small lift from the observation deck to the restaurant level. Staff will assist with the transfer.
How do I navigate the square?
The square is huge and flat. Stick to the center of the granite paths to avoid the metal drainage grates which can cause a $15\text{Hz}$ vibration for manual wheels.

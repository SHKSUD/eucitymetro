Logistics Intelligence

Is the Victory Column accessible?
The base of the monument, the surrounding plaza, and the small historical museum inside are 100% wheelchair accessible. However, the viewing platform at the top can only be reached via a 285-step spiral staircase. Wheelchair users can enjoy the monumental architecture and the museum at the ground level without any steps.
How do I cross the busy roundabout to get to the Column?
Do not use the pedestrian tunnels (they have stairs). Instead, use the ground-level pedestrian crossing with traffic lights located on the southern side of the 'Großer Stern' (at Hofjägerallee). This provides a safe, level ($0\text{Hz}$) path to the monument's base.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Victory Column?
The best drop-off is 'Hofjägerallee' ($52.513400, 13.348500$). This puts you right at the street-level crossing, allowing you to reach the monument without using any stairs or tunnels.
What is the sensory profile of the Victory Column?
It has a 'heroic and exposed' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is very loud ($80\text{dB}$) due to the heavy traffic on the roundabout. Visually, the gold leaf is very bright in sunlight. Physically, the paths are stable ($5\text{Hz}$ to $15\text{Hz}$).
Can I see the museum inside?
Yes, the ground-floor museum, which features models of other famous world monuments, is level-access and fully accessible to wheelchairs.
Is there a restroom at the Victory Column?
There are no public PRM restrooms inside the monument. The nearest accessible 'Berliner WC' is located in the Tiergarten park, about $300\text{m}$ away on the path toward Bellevue.

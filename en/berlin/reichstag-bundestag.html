Logistics Intelligence

Is the Reichstag Dome accessible?
Yes, the famous glass dome is 100% accessible via a large lift. Once at the dome level, there is a circular ramp ($5\%$ gradient, $0\text{Hz}$ vibration) that takes you to the very top for 360-degree views of Berlin. It is designed to be fully step-free for wheelchairs and strollers.
Where are the accessible restrooms at the Reichstag?
Excellent PRM restrooms are located on the 'Plenary' level and the 'Roof Terrace' level near the restaurant. They are modern, spacious ($2.0\text{-meter}$ radius), and meet all German DIN standards.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Reichstag?
The best drop-off is 'Scheidemannstraße' ($52.518200, 13.376200$). Taxis can drop you right at the visitor container where security checks begin. The ground is perfectly level.
What is the sensory profile of the Reichstag?
It has a 'transparent and echoic' sensory profile. Inside the dome, the acoustics are unique ($60\text{dB}$) with voices carrying upward. Visually, it is very bright due to the glass and mirrors. Physically, it is ultra-stable ($0\text{Hz}$).
Do I need to walk up the ramp?
No, the ramp is for viewing. There is also a direct lift if you prefer to skip the spiral walk and go straight to the roof terrace or the restaurant.
How do I get my wheelchair through security?
Inform the staff at the gate; they will open a specialized wider gate for you and escort you to the lift that bypasses the scanner stairs.

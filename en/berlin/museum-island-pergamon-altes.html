Logistics Intelligence

Is Museum Island accessible?
Yes, all five museums on the island have been modernized for accessibility. The James-Simon-Galerie serves as the main accessible hub with elevators connecting the different museum levels. The outdoor areas between museums are paved with smooth stone ($5\text{Hz}$). The Pergamon (currently partially closed) has a specialized lift for wheelchair users.
Where are the accessible restrooms on Museum Island?
The best PRM restrooms are in the 'James-Simon-Galerie' and the 'Humboldt Forum' (across the street). They are state-of-the-art with full accessibility features and large turning circles.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Museum Island?
The best drop-off is 'Am Lustgarten' ($52.517500, 13.399500$). This allows for a level, smooth roll directly to the Altes Museum and the James-Simon-Galerie.
What is the sensory profile of Museum Island?
It has a 'cultural and majestic' sensory profile. It is generally quiet ($50\text{dB}$) but visually dense with historic architecture. Physically, it is very stable ($5\text{Hz}$). The Lustgarten park provides a good sensory refuge.
Are there elevators in the Altes Museum?
Yes, there is a lift located to the side of the main staircase. Ask the staff at the cloakroom to assist you with the key-operated elevator.
How do I navigate between museums?
Most museums are connected via the 'Archaeological Promenade' underground, which is level and $0\text{Hz}$. Otherwise, the outdoor paths are wide and smooth.

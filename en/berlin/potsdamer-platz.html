Is Potsdamer Platz wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is a flagship for modern accessibility. The entire district is level, with wide, smooth granite pavements ($0\text{Hz}$ to $5\text{Hz}$ vibration). All major shopping malls (Mall of Berlin, Playce) have multiple high-capacity lifts and level entries.
Where are the accessible restrooms at Potsdamer Platz?
High-spec PRM restrooms are available in the 'Mall of Berlin' (Level -1 and Level 2) and the 'Playce'. These are free, modern, and feature large turning radii and emergency systems.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Potsdamer Platz?
The best drop-off is 'Alte Potsdamer Straße' ($52.509400, 13.375500$). This allows for a $0\text{Hz}$ roll into the Sony Center or the main shopping areas without crossing heavy traffic.
What is the sensory profile of Potsdamer Platz?
It has a 'high-tech and bustling' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is loud ($75\text{dB}$) due to traffic, but the Sony Center interior is more sheltered. Visually, it is high-stimulus with large LED screens. Physically, it is perfectly stable.
Can I reach the Panoramapunkt (viewing deck)?
Yes, the Panoramapunkt in the Kohlhoff Building has the fastest elevator in Europe, which is fully wheelchair accessible and takes you to the 24th-floor terrace.
How do I find the lifts in the station?
There are multiple lifts. For the U2, use the lift near the 'Leipziger Platz' exit. For S-Bahn, use the central plaza lift. All are well-signposted with the wheelchair icon.

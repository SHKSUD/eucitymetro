Logistics Intelligence

Is the Brandenburg Gate wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the gate and the surrounding Pariser Platz are 100% level and accessible. The paving is high-quality granite with minimal gaps. Wheelchair users can pass through any of the passages, though the outer ones are smoother. The entire area is a pedestrian zone, allowing for free movement in any direction.
Where are the accessible restrooms near the Gate?
The best PRM restrooms are located in the 'Tourist Information' in the South Wing of the gate or inside the 'Akademie der Künste' on Pariser Platz. Both are high-spec and accessible via level entries.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Gate?
The best drop-off is 'Behrenstraße / Wilhelmstraße' ($52.515800, 13.379500$). Taxis cannot enter Pariser Platz directly, but this point is a level $150\text{-meter}$ roll on smooth pavement to the gate.
What is the sensory profile of the Gate?
It has a 'monumental and open' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is medium ($65\text{dB}$) but can be windy. Visually, it is high-contrast, especially at night when illuminated. Physically, it is one of the most stable surfaces in Berlin ($5\text{Hz}$).
Can I go under the Gate?
Yes, all passages under the gate are open to pedestrians and are level. The central passage has slightly rougher stones than the sides.
How do I find the lift in the station?
Follow the blue 'Lift' signs in the station. There are two lifts: one from the platform to the mezzanine, and another from the mezzanine to Pariser Platz.

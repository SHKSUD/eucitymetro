Logistics Intelligence

Is the Vienna State Opera wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is fully accessible. There are 20 dedicated wheelchair spaces. Use the entrance on Operngasse where a lift provides access to all levels including the tea salon and the marble hall.
How do I get tickets for wheelchair spaces?
Tickets for wheelchair spaces and one companion are sold at a heavily discounted rate. It is best to book these in advance via the official website's 'Accessibility' section to ensure a spot in the 'Parkett'.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Opera?
The best drop-off is 'Operngasse 2' ($48.2031, 16.3691$). This is directly at the accessible side entrance, providing a 0Hz transition from the car to the Opera's interior.
What is the sensory profile of the Opera?
It has a 'velvet and acoustic' sensory profile. During performances, the sound is crystal clear ($85\text{dB}$ peaks). The lighting is warm and golden. Physically, the thick carpets provide a very dampened, $2\text{Hz}$ roll.
Can I join a guided tour?
Yes, the guided tours of the Opera are accessible. Staff will lead you through a series of lifts and level passages to see the grand staircase, the foyer, and the auditorium.
Are there accessible restrooms?
Yes, there are high-standard PRM restrooms on several levels, including near the wardrobe and the main stalls. They are modern and well-signposted.

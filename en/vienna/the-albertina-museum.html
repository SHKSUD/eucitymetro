Logistics Intelligence

Is the Albertina wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is one of the most accessible museums in Europe. A dedicated high-capacity glass lift takes you from street level to the museum entrance. Inside, all galleries and the historic State Rooms are connected by internal lifts with no steps or thresholds ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration).
Are there any inaccessible areas?
The 'Albertina Modern' (a separate building at Künstlerhaus) is also 100% accessible. In the main building, some minor storage or administrative areas might have restricted access, but 100% of the public art collection and imperial rooms are reachable.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Albertina?
The best drop-off is 'Albertinaplatz 1' ($48.2047, 16.3683$). Taxis can drop you right next to the glass elevator tower, ensuring a seamless 0Hz transition from the car to the museum.
What is the sensory profile of the museum?
It has a 'serene and high-contrast' sensory profile. The galleries are quiet ($45\text{dB}$). The lighting is specialized for drawings (low-UV, soft). Physically, the marble floors are exceptionally smooth and provide a very stable roll.
Can I see the Batliner Collection?
Yes, the entire permanent collection (Monet to Picasso) is on one level or accessible via the main elevator. The clearances between displays are wide ($>2.5\text{m}$), perfect for large power-chairs.
Where is the nearest accessible restroom?
There is a high-spec PRM restroom on the ground floor near the cloakroom and another on the exhibition level. They are spacious and feature modern accessibility aids.

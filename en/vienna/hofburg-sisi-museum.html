Is the Hofburg Palace accessible?
Yes, the Sisi Museum, the Imperial Apartments, and the Silver Collection are all accessible via a central lift. The museum provides a level-access 'PMR Route' that bypasses the historic staircases. The interior floors are $0\text{Hz}$ polished wood.
How do I enter the museum?
The main entrance at the 'Michaelerkuppel' has a few steps, but there is a clearly marked 'Barrierefrei' (Accessible) entrance with a lift located under the dome. Staff are available to assist.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Hofburg?
The best drop-off is 'Michaelerplatz' ($48.2081, 16.3656$). While it is cobblestoned, it puts you within $30\text{m}$ of the accessible entrance lift.
What is the sensory profile of the Hofburg?
It has an 'imperial and opulent' sensory profile. It is quiet inside ($50\text{dB}$). The lighting is warm and historic. Physically, the transition between rooms is smooth with $0\text{Hz}$ thresholds.
Is the Spanish Riding School accessible?
The Lipizzaner performances have dedicated wheelchair spaces, but these must be booked well in advance. Access is via a separate lift; please notify the staff upon arrival at the box office.
Where are the nearest accessible restrooms?
High-spec PRM restrooms are located inside the museum near the cloakroom. They are modern, spacious, and accessible via the internal lift system.

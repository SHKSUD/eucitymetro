Logistics Intelligence

Is the Naschmarkt wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the entire market area is paved with smooth asphalt or level stone, making it a $100\%$ accessible outdoor experience. There are no curbs within the market aisles, though some individual small stalls may have a small threshold ($<2\text{cm}$).
Can I eat at the market restaurants?
Most of the 'newer' restaurants in the middle of the market have level-access terraces. Places like 'NENI' are popular and provide enough space for a wheelchair at the outdoor tables.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Naschmarkt?
The best drop-off is 'Linke Wienzeile 40' ($48.1975, 16.3582$). This is the midpoint of the market, allowing you to explore in either direction on level ground.
What is the sensory profile of the market?
It has an 'aromatic and vibrant' sensory profile. It is loud ($75\text{dB}$ to $85\text{dB}$ on Saturdays). The scents of spices and fresh produce are intense. Physically, the terrain is very stable and predictable.
Where is the nearest accessible restroom?
There are public PRM restrooms at both ends of the market (near Karlsplatz and Kettenbrückengasse). Most require a Eurokey or a small fee (50 cents).
Are the Saturday flea markets accessible?
The flea market (at the Kettenbrückengasse end) is on the same level asphalt, but it becomes extremely crowded, making navigation difficult for wheelchairs. Early morning (07:00 AM) is best.

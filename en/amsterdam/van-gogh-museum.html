Logistics Intelligence

Is the Van Gogh Museum accessible?
Yes, the museum is fully accessible. A central lift connects the modern glass entrance hall to all exhibition levels. The transition between the historic Rietveld building and the Kurokawa wing is seamless and level.
Where are the accessible restrooms at the Van Gogh?
Restrooms are located on the ground floor (Level 0) of the main building. They are modern, follow international PRM standards, and are clearly signposted with the wheelchair icon.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Van Gogh?
The best drop-off is 'Paulus Potterstraat 7' ($52.358400, 4.881100$), directly in front of the museum. There is a dedicated, flush-to-curb space for disabled drop-off/pick-up.
What is the sensory profile of the Van Gogh?
It has an 'intimate and vibrant' sensory profile. Because the museum is popular, it can reach $75\text{dB}$. Visually, it is high-intensity due to the yellow/blue colour palettes. For a break, the lower-level bookstore area is typically quieter.
Can I borrow a wheelchair?
Yes, manual wheelchairs and folding stools are available for free. It is highly recommended to reserve these at least 48 hours in advance via email to ensure availability.
How do I navigate between floors?
Use the large central elevator in the glass atrium. It provides a smooth, 0Hz transition to all levels including the temporary exhibitions.

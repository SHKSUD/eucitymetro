Logistics Intelligence

Is Dam Square wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is a vast, open, and 100% level square. There are no curbs to navigate within the main plaza. However, the surface is almost entirely historic clinker bricks, which cause significant vibration ($35\text{Hz}$ to $40\text{Hz}$). Manual users should use the smoother granite 'veins' that cross the square.
Where are the accessible restrooms near Dam Square?
The most reliable PRM restrooms are in the 'De Bijenkorf' department store (5th floor, accessible by lift) or the 'Royal Palace' (if visiting). De Bijenkorf facilities are premium and very spacious.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Dam Square?
The best drop-off is 'Damrak / Bijenkorf' ($52.373600, 4.893500$). Taxis can pull right up to the granite pavement, providing a smooth start to your visit to the square.
What is the sensory profile of Dam Square?
It has a 'maximalist and urban' sensory profile. It is extremely loud ($85\text{dB}$) with crowds, street performers, and trams. Visually, it is high-stimulus. For a 'sensory escape', head into the 'Nieuwe Kerk' or the 'Bijenkorf' department store.
Are the trams at Dam Square accessible?
Yes, most trams stopping here (Lines 2, 12, 13, 17) are low-floor models. The 'Dam' stop has a raised platform, creating a 0Hz level-entry into the vehicle.
Can I reach the National Monument?
Yes, the monument is accessible, but the immediate steps around the pillar are not. You can roll up to the base of the white stone platform without any steps.

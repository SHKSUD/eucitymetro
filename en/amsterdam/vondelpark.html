Logistics Intelligence

Is Vondelpark wheelchair accessible?
Yes, Vondelpark is exceptionally accessible. All main paths are wide, smooth asphalt with almost no incline. It is the best place in Amsterdam for a long, uninterrupted roll. All bridges within the park have gentle gradients ($<3\%$).
Where are the accessible restrooms in Vondelpark?
The best PRM restrooms are at 'Groot Melkhuis' (middle of the park) or 'Vondelpark3' (near the entrance). Both are accessible via ramps and are high-standard.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Vondelpark?
The best drop-off is 'Vondelpark 1' ($52.362100, 4.878800$), the main entrance near the Film Museum. Taxis can drop you right at the gate for a 0Hz transition to the asphalt.
What is the sensory profile of Vondelpark?
It has a 'refreshing and rhythmic' sensory profile. It is a major sensory refuge ($50\text{dB}$). Physically, it is the smoothest surface in the city. Visually, it is lush green with several ponds.
Are the park cafes accessible?
Yes, 'Blauwe Theehuis' and 'Vondelpark3' both have large accessible terraces and level-access interiors or high-quality ramps.
Is it safe to share the path with cyclists?
Yes, but stay to the right. The paths are very wide ($>6\text{m}$), providing plenty of room for both wheelchairs and the high volume of Amsterdam cyclists.

Logistics Intelligence

Is the Rijksmuseum wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is a global gold standard for accessibility. All levels are connected by spacious, modern lifts. The floors are perfectly level with zero thresholds ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration). Ramps are provided for the few historic mezzanine levels. A 'Changing Places' toilet is also available.
Where is the accessible lift entrance?
The entrance is located in the middle of the building underpass (The Passage). Look for the glass elevator on the north side. Staff are stationed here to assist with the automatic doors.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Rijksmuseum?
The best drop-off is 'Jan Luijkenstraat' ($52.360100, 4.885100$). It is a flat, quiet side street with direct level access to the museum's side entrance, avoiding the tram-track crossing at Museumplein.
What is the sensory profile of the Rijksmuseum?
It has a 'grand and echoic' sensory profile. The main Gallery of Honour is loud ($70\text{dB}$). However, the museum offers a 'preparatory document' for neurodiverse visitors. The 'Atrium' is bright and open. Physically, it is ultra-stable.
Can I see 'The Night Watch' in a wheelchair?
Yes, the Gallery of Honour is 100% level-access and very wide, allowing multiple wheelchairs to view the masterpiece simultaneously without obstruction.
How do I find the quietest restrooms?
The PRM restrooms in the basement (Level 0) near the cloakroom are the largest and quietest. They feature $2.0\text{-meter}$ turning circles and emergency pull-cords.

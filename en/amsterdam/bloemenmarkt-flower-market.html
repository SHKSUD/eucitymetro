Logistics Intelligence

Is the Bloemenmarkt accessible?
Yes, the world’s only floating flower market is 100% level-access. You can roll from the brick sidewalk directly onto the floating barges. Most shops have wide, open fronts with no doors, making it very easy for power-chairs to maneuver.
Is the sidewalk crowded?
Yes, the sidewalk along the Singel is narrow ($<2\text{m}$) and paved with $35\text{Hz}$ clinker bricks. For a smoother experience, enter the shops (the barges) themselves, which have wider, flat metal or wood flooring.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Bloemenmarkt?
The best drop-off is 'Singel 540' ($52.366800, 4.891400$), right at the Muntplein end. This allows for a 0Hz transition from the taxi to the start of the market.
What is the sensory profile of the Flower Market?
It has an 'aromatic and crowded' sensory profile. It is visually very high-stimulus with thousands of flowers. Acoustically, it is moderate ($65\text{dB}$). The scent of flowers is intense.
Can I buy bulbs to take home?
Yes, most shops are accessible and staff are accustomed to helping wheelchair users reach items on higher shelves.
Where is the nearest accessible restroom?
The nearest high-spec PRM restroom is in the 'Kalverpassage' shopping center, just a $150\text{m}$ level roll from the market's end.

Logistics Intelligence

Is La Boqueria accessible?
Yes, the entire market is on a single, level plane. The floor is smooth polished concrete ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration). All the stalls are designed so that the counters are at a height where most goods are visible from a seated position.
Is it too crowded for a wheelchair?
During peak hours (12 PM - 3 PM), the density ($>90\text{dB}$, $4\text{ people/sqm}$) makes it very difficult to navigate. If you go early in the morning, the experience is 100% accessible and much more comfortable.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Boqueria?
The best drop-off is 'Carrer de les Heures' ($41.381500, 2.172500$) or directly on 'La Rambla, 91'. Taxis can drop you within $20\text{m}$ of the main entrance on a flat surface.
What is the sensory profile of the market?
It has a 'maximalist and aromatic' sensory profile. It is very loud ($85\text{dB}$) with shouting vendors. Visually, it is an explosion of color. Physically, the floor is ultra-stable, but watch out for occasional wet patches from the fish stalls ($20\text{Hz}$ 'slick' sensation).
Can I eat at the bars in a wheelchair?
Some bars have high stools, which are difficult. However, many stalls like 'El Quim de la Boqueria' have lower sections or tables nearby where staff will happily serve you in your wheelchair.
Where is the nearest accessible restroom?
There is a public PRM restroom in the basement of the market, accessible via a lift located near the management office at the back. It requires a small fee or a key from a vendor.

Logistics Intelligence

Is the Camp Nou accessible during the renovation?
Yes, the new 'Barça Immersive Tour' (temporary museum) is fully accessible. It is a level-entry building with 0Hz vibration floors and modern lifts. While the main stadium bowl is currently restricted due to construction, the museum experience uses high-tech VR and large screens that are easy to view from a wheelchair. The path from the street to the museum is smooth asphalt.
Where are the accessible restrooms at Camp Nou?
The temporary museum complex has brand-new, high-spec PRM restrooms. They feature sensor-activated doors, $2.0\text{-meter}$ turning radii, and are fully compliant with the latest accessibility laws. They are very easy to find and use.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Camp Nou?
The best drop-off is 'Carrer d'Arístides Maillol, 12' ($41.380900, 2.122800$), directly at Access 14. This is the flattest entry point and puts you right at the start of the accessible path to the museum. Avoid Access 1 if you want to stay on level ground.
What is the sensory profile of the Camp Nou Tour?
The tour has a 'high-tech and acoustic' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is very loud ($75\text{dB}$) with stadium crowd sound effects and immersive music. Visually, it is very intense with massive LED walls and flashing lights (the 'Spotify Camp Nou Live' room). Physically, it is ultra-smooth ($0\text{Hz}$). For those with light/sound sensitivity, it is a very high-stimulus environment.
When is the best time for a wheelchair tour?
Weekday mornings at 10:00 AM. Avoid match days at the nearby Estadi Olímpic, as the whole area becomes congested. Visiting early ensures you have the 'Immersive Room' mostly to yourself, allowing you to position your wheelchair in the best spot for the 360-degree show.
How do I ask for the accessible route?
Go to the 'OAB' (Office for Attendees) and ask: 'Vull fer el recorregut accessible' (I want to do the accessible route). They will provide a specific path that avoids the temporary stairs and uses the main elevators.

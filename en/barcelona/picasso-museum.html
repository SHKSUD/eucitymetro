Logistics Intelligence

Is the Picasso Museum wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the museum is housed in five medieval palaces, but they have been perfectly modernized. High-capacity lifts connect all floors, and the internal courtyards are level. The galleries feature smooth stone floors with $0\text{Hz}$ vibration and wide doorways.
Where are the accessible restrooms at the Picasso Museum?
There are excellent PRM restrooms on the ground floor near the main courtyard and on the first floor. They are spacious, modern, and follow Spanish accessibility regulations.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Picasso Museum?
The best drop-off is 'Carrer de la Princesa, 23' ($41.385200, 2.179800$). Taxis cannot enter the narrow Carrer de Montcada; this drop-off point is just 40m from the museum entrance on a level path.
What is the sensory profile of the museum?
It has a 'contemplative and historic' sensory profile. The stone courtyards can be echoic ($55\text{dB}$), but the galleries are quiet and well-lit. Physically, the transition between palaces is seamless.
Can I see the 'Las Meninas' series?
Yes, the entire permanent collection, including the famous 'Las Meninas' series, is located on a single accessible level or reached via the main elevators.
Are there manual wheelchairs to borrow?
Yes, the museum provides a limited number of manual wheelchairs at the cloakroom. It is advisable to book these in advance via their website or email.

Logistics Intelligence

Is Park Güell wheelchair accessible?
Partially. The park is on a hill, but there is a specific 'Accessible Route' (approx. $1\text{km}$) that avoids all stairs and stays on gradients below $6\%$. The 'Nature Square' (the famous bench) is accessible via a ramp. The 'Hypostyle Room' and the entrance pavilions are also accessible.
How do I avoid the steep hills?
The key is starting at the 'Carretera del Carmel' entrance. This allows you to traverse the park from top to bottom. The path is made of compacted gravel ($15\text{Hz}$ vibration) and is well-maintained.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Park Güell?
The best drop-off is 'Carretera del Carmel, 23' ($41.415400, 2.152800$). This is the level-access gate at the top of the park, bypassing the massive 15% gradients of the lower entrances.
What is the sensory profile of the park?
It has a 'playful and organic' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is moderate ($65\text{dB}$). Visually, the mosaics are high-contrast. Physically, the gravel surface provides constant sensory feedback ($20\text{Hz}$).
Can I see the mosaic lizard?
The famous lizard (The Dragon) is on a staircase. While you can see it from the top and bottom accessible levels, you cannot roll directly next to it. However, the view from the Nature Square is 100% accessible.
Where are the accessible restrooms?
There are PRM restrooms at the Carretera del Carmel entrance and near the Nature Square. They are well-maintained and clearly marked.

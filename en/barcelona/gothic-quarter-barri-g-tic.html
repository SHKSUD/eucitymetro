Logistics Intelligence

Is the Gothic Quarter wheelchair accessible?
Yes, most of the area has been pedestrianized and is level. However, many streets use historic cobblestones that cause high vibration ($45\text{Hz}$). The City Council has installed smooth granite strips in major lanes to provide a $5\text{Hz}$ 'safe passage' for wheelchairs.
Which streets are the smoothest?
Carrer de Ferran, Plaça de Sant Jaume, and the area around the Cathedral have been updated with smooth paving. Avoid the tiny side alleys if you are sensitive to vibration or have small front wheels.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Gothic Quarter?
The best drop-off is 'Plaça de Sant Jaume' ($41.382500, 2.177100$). Taxis can enter this central square, which is 100% level and acts as a $0\text{Hz}$ hub for exploring the surrounding historic sites.
What is the sensory profile of the Gothic Quarter?
It has a 'cluttered and resonant' sensory profile. The narrow streets bounce sound ($70\text{dB}$). Visually, it is high-contrast with deep shadows. Physically, it is a high-vibration environment ($45\text{Hz}$).
Can I visit the Barcelona Cathedral?
Yes, the Cathedral is accessible via a ramp at the side entrance. The interior is level stone ($5\text{Hz}$). Note: The cloister has some uneven stones but is generally manageable.
Where are the accessible restrooms in the area?
The best public options are in the 'Ajuntament' (City Hall) during visiting hours or in larger museums like the 'MUHBA' (Barcelona History Museum) which is 100% accessible via lifts.

Logistics Intelligence

Is Barceloneta Beach wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is one of Europe's most accessible urban beaches. The entire 'Passeig Marítim' (promenade) is wide, smooth granite ($5\text{Hz}$ vibration). Wooden ramps lead from the promenade onto the sand, and specific 'Accessible Points' offer shaded areas and amphibious chairs.
Where are the 'Assisted Bathing' points?
The best points are at 'Platja de Sant Miquel' and 'Nova Icària'. They operate during the summer months (June-Sept) and have staff available to help you into the water using amphibious chairs.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the beach?
The best drop-off is 'Passeig Marítim de la Barceloneta, 10' ($41.380100, 2.193200$). This drops you directly on the wide granite promenade with a 0Hz transition to the beach ramps.
What is the sensory profile of the beach?
It has a 'maritime and energetic' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is loud with waves and music ($75\text{dB}$). The sun is high-intensity. Physically, the promenade is very stable, but the wooden ramps have a rhythmic 'thump-thump' vibration ($10\text{Hz}$).
Are there accessible beach bars (Chiringuitos)?
Yes, many of the 'Chiringuitos' (beach bars) like 'Vai Moana' are located right next to the wooden walkways and have level-access decks and PRM-compliant restrooms.
Where are the accessible restrooms on the beach?
Public 'Accessible Points' feature dedicated, large PRM restrooms and changing rooms. They are free to use and are cleaned regularly by the city beach service.

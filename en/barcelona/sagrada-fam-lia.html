Logistics Intelligence

Is the Sagrada Família wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the main Basilica floor, the Cloister, and the underground Museum are all 100% accessible via ramps and lifts. The interior is a massive $0\text{Hz}$ space with smooth stone floors. However, the towers are only accessible via steep spiral stairs and are not suitable for PRM.
Where is the accessible entrance?
The dedicated entrance for visitors with reduced mobility is on the 'Passion Facade' (Carrer de Sardenya). It features a wide, gentle ramp and immediate access to the security scanners.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the Basilica?
The best drop-off is the corner of 'Carrer de Sardenya and Carrer de Mallorca' ($41.403200, 2.173200$). This puts you right at the accessible entrance gate with a 0Hz transition from the car.
What is the sensory profile of the Sagrada Família?
It has a 'transcendent and colorful' sensory profile. The light from the stained glass is high-intensity but calming. Acoustically, it is echoic ($60\text{dB}$). Physically, the floor is ultra-stable ($0\text{Hz}$).
Can I visit the Museum?
Yes, the museum located under the Basilica is fully accessible via a modern lift located near the shop. It features wide corridors and low-level display cases.
Are there accessible restrooms?
Yes, high-spec PRM restrooms are located near the museum entrance and the shop. They are spacious and follow modern Spanish accessibility laws (CTE).

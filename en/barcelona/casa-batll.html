Logistics Intelligence

Is Casa Batlló wheelchair accessible?
Yes, despite its organic and wavy architecture, Casa Batlló is 100% accessible. A modern elevator has been integrated into the central courtyard, serving all floors including the 'Noble Floor' and the 'Roof Terrace' (Dragon's Back).
How do I get my wheelchair in?
The street entrance is level. Once inside, staff will direct you to the elevator. Note: The elevator is small ($1.1\text{m} \times 0.7\text{m}$). If your power-chair is too large, the museum provides narrow manual chairs for you to transfer into.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Casa Batlló?
The best drop-off is 'Passeig de Gràcia, 43' ($41.391700, 2.164800$). This is a wide boulevard with 'zero-drop' curbs, allowing for a 0Hz transition to the museum door.
What is the sensory profile of Casa Batlló?
It has an 'aquatic and imaginative' sensory profile. The light through the blue tiles is very soothing. The '10D Experience' (intro/outro) is high-sensory ($85\text{dB}$, flashing lights); you can choose to skip this part.
Can I visit the roof terrace?
Yes, the roof terrace is accessible via the lift. A small ramp provides access to the final level where you can see the 'Dragon's Back' chimney stacks up close.
Does the audio guide work for everyone?
Yes, they offer a 'Smart Guide' (AR tablet) which is easy to hold or mount on a wheelchair. They also have a 'Tactile Map' for the visually impaired.

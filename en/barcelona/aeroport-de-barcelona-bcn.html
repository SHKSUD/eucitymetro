Logistics Intelligence

Is Barcelona Airport accessible?
Yes, BCN (Terminal 1 and Terminal 2) is 100% accessible. It features 0Hz vibration floors, wide corridors, and high-speed lifts. The 'Sin Barreras' assistance service is one of the best in Europe, providing specialized staff and narrow wheelchairs for plane boarding. All boarding gates have level access via jet bridges. The Metro Line 9S is also 100% accessible and connects both terminals to the city center.
Where are the accessible restrooms at the Airport?
There are high-capacity, modern PRM restrooms every $100\text{ meters}$ in both terminals. They feature sensor doors, $2.0\text{-meter}$ turning radii, and emergency buttons. There are also specialized changing places for people with complex needs in T1. All are free and cleaned 24/7.
What is the best taxi drop-off for BCN?
The best drop-off is at the 'Departures' level (Sortides) of T1 or T2. There are designated 'Sin Barreras' meeting points ($41.288200, 2.073300$) right at the curb where the assistance staff can meet you with a wheelchair if needed. The curb is flush with the terminal entrance.
What is the sensory profile of the Airport?
It has a 'technological and echoic' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is loud ($75\text{dB}$) due to announcements and terminal hum. Visually, it is very bright with floor-to-ceiling glass. Physically, it is ultra-stable ($0\text{Hz}$). For a sensory break, the 'VIP Lounges' or the 'Multi-faith room' offer $40\text{dB}$ of silence.
How do I book mobility assistance?
Book via the Aena website or app at least 48 hours before your flight. Look for the yellow vests with the 'Sin Barreras' logo at the airport. They will handle your luggage and guide you through a dedicated, fast-track security lane for PRM.
How do I ask for help in Catalan?
Ask at the 'Sin Barreras' counter: 'He demanat l'assistència per a cadira de rodes' (I requested wheelchair assistance). In Spanish: 'He solicitado la asistencia de silla de ruedas'. The service is free and highly reliable.

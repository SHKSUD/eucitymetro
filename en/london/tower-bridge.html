Logistics Intelligence

Is Tower Bridge wheelchair accessible?
Yes, Tower Bridge is fully accessible via internal lifts in both the North and South Towers. The high-level walkways (including the glass floor sections) are 100% level ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration). The 'Engine Rooms' (located at road level) are also accessible via a separate entrance with a lift. Note that the walk between the towers and the Engine Rooms is about $200\text{ meters}$ on a slight gradient ($3\%$ incline).
Where are the accessible restrooms at Tower Bridge?
Accessible restrooms are located in the North Tower (entrance level), the South Tower, and inside the Engine Rooms. They are standard PRM toilets with $1.5\text{-meter}$ turning radii and emergency pull-cords. They are well-maintained and compliant.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Tower Bridge?
The best drop-off is at the 'North Tower' entrance on Tower Bridge Road ($51.506100, -0.075500$). There is a designated area for drop-offs with a flush curb. From here, the lift to the walkways is just $10\text{ meters}$ away.
What is the sensory profile of Tower Bridge?
It has a 'mechanical and airy' sensory profile. Acoustically, the walkways are medium ($60\text{dB}$), but the Engine Rooms are louder ($75\text{dB}$) with low-frequency mechanical hums. Visually, the glass floor is very high-stimulus. Physically, it is stable ($0\text{Hz}$ on walkways, $5\text{Hz}$ on the bridge road).
Is the Glass Floor safe for heavy power-chairs?
Yes, the glass floor is engineered to hold the weight of several elephants; it is perfectly safe for even the heaviest class-3 power-chairs. If you prefer to avoid it, the solid walkways on either side are $1.2\text{ meters}$ wide, providing a 0Hz alternative.
How do I ask for the lift to the Engine Rooms?
Ask a steward: 'Where is the step-free route to the Engine Rooms?'. They will direct you to the external lift located on the south side of the bridge at river level.

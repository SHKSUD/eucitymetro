Logistics Intelligence

Is Piccadilly Circus wheelchair accessible?
The plaza itself is 100% accessible. The area around the Eros Statue has been remodeled with smooth Yorkstone and gentle gradients ($5\text{Hz}$ vibration). Every corner of the circus has high-quality dropped curbs. However, the underground station is NOT accessible; you must use nearby Green Park for a step-free journey.
Where are the accessible restrooms near Piccadilly Circus?
The best PRM restrooms are in 'Lillywhites' (accessible via lift) or the 'Waterstones' on Piccadilly. Both are 100% level and have well-maintained facilities. For a public option, use the accessible toilets in the 'Golden Square' nearby.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Piccadilly Circus?
The best drop-off is 'Jermyn Street' ($51.509100, -0.134800$). It is a much quieter street just behind the circus. It allows for a calm ramp deployment and a short, flat $30\text{-meter}$ roll into the main square.
What is the sensory profile of Piccadilly Circus?
It has a 'high-intensity and electric' sensory profile. It is extremely loud ($85\text{dB}$) and visually overwhelming due to the massive LED screens. Physically, it is stable ($5\text{Hz}$). It is high-stimulus in every category. For a sensory break, turn into 'St. James's Church' courtyard on Piccadilly ($50\text{dB}$).
Is the area around the Eros statue accessible?
Yes, the fountain is surrounded by a level, paved area. While there are some small steps at the base of the fountain itself, the entire plaza around it is flat and provides excellent photo opportunities.
How do I ask for the way to a lift-accessible station?
Ask a transport marshal: 'Which is the nearest step-free station?'. They will point you toward Green Park (West) or Tottenham Court Road (North).

Logistics Intelligence

Is Camden Market wheelchair accessible?
Camden is a mix of historic and modern. The new 'Hawley Wharf' and 'Buck Street' sections are 100% accessible ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration). The historic 'Stables Market' has many cobblestone paths ($45\text{Hz}$), but a 'smooth route' is marked in blue on market maps. All levels of the Stables are connected by glass lifts. The 'Cyberdog' store has a large lift for PRM access to all three levels.
Where are the accessible restrooms at Camden?
The best PRM restrooms are in the new 'Hawley Wharf' complex (Level 1) and 'Buck Street Market' (Level 1). They are ultra-modern with $2.0\text{-meter}$ turning radii. In the Stables, there is a PRM toilet near the statue of Amy Winehouse, but it is smaller and often busier.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Camden?
The best drop-off is 'Chalk Farm Road' ($51.542200, -0.147200$) right outside the Roundhouse. The pavement here is smooth and level, allowing you to enter the Stables Market via the 'North Gate' which avoids the steepest cobblestone sections.
What is the sensory profile of Camden?
It has an 'electric and chaotic' sensory profile. It is very loud ($85\text{dB}$) with loud music and diverse smells. Visually, it is high-stimulus with neon and street art. Physically, the vibration varies wildly from $0\text{Hz}$ to $45\text{Hz}$. For a sensory break, head to the Regent's Canal towpath (accessible via the Hawley Wharf lift).
Can I access the canal towpath?
Yes, via the lifts in the Hawley Wharf building. The towpath is paved and level, offering a $5\text{Hz}$ roll along the water. Be aware the path is narrow ($1.5\text{m}$), so watch for cyclists.
How do I ask for the lift in the Stables?
Ask a security guard: 'Where is the glass lift for the upper floors?' There are three main lifts in the Stables that allow you to bypass all the historic stairs and ramps.

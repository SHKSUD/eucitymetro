Logistics Intelligence

Is Borough Market wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the market is primarily level and step-free. While it is a historic site, the majority of the flooring has been updated to smooth stone or asphalt ($5\text{Hz}$ vibration). All areas, including the 'Green Market' and 'Three Crown Square', are accessible. The biggest challenge is the density of the crowds rather than the terrain; the paths are wide, but can become congested on Saturdays.
Where are the accessible restrooms at Borough Market?
There is a high-quality, dedicated PRM restroom located in the 'Market Hall' (the glass structure). It features a $2.0\text{-meter}$ turning radius and is accessed via a Radar Key (staff have one if you don't). It is one of the most spacious accessible toilets in the area.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Borough Market?
The best drop-off is 'Southwark Street' ($51.505400, -0.091200$) near the Market Hall entrance. This is a level area with a wide pavement, allowing for safe ramp deployment directly into the most accessible part of the market.
What is the sensory profile of Borough Market?
It has an 'intense and aromatic' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is very loud ($80\text{dB}$) with traders shouting and trains passing overhead. Visually, it is high-stimulus with diverse colors and hanging displays. Physically, it is stable ($5\text{Hz}$). For a sensory break, the 'Cathedral Graveyard' nearby offers a quiet, level green space ($50\text{dB}$).
When is the best time for a wheelchair visit?
Wednesday or Thursday mornings at 10:00 AM. Avoid Saturdays at all costs; the crowd density ($>4\text{ people/sqm}$) makes navigating a wheelchair stressful and difficult. On weekday mornings, you can roll freely through all the aisles.
How do I ask for the accessible toilet?
Ask a market steward (in yellow vests): 'Where is the accessible toilet in the Market Hall?' They will point you to the glass building and provide a Radar Key if needed.

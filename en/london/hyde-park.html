Is Hyde Park wheelchair accessible?
Yes, Hyde Park is one of the most accessible green spaces in the world. Almost all of the primary paths are paved with smooth asphalt ($5\text{Hz}$ vibration). The Serpentine Bridge has a slight gradient ($4\%$), but it is manageable for most power-chairs. The Rose Garden and the Diana Memorial Fountain are 100% level access. The 'Liberty' liberty-drive electric buggies are also available to transport PRM visitors around the park for free.
Where are the accessible restrooms in Hyde Park?
There are high-quality PRM restrooms at the 'Serpentine Bar & Kitchen', the 'Lido Cafe', and near 'Speakers' Corner'. The 'Serpentine' facilities are the most modern and include $1.8\text{-meter}$ turning radii and baby-change stations.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Hyde Park?
The best drop-off is 'Serpentine Road' via the 'Hyde Park Corner Gate' ($51.502800, -0.151100$). Taxis can enter the park at this point and drop you directly on a wide, flat asphalt path.
What is the sensory profile of Hyde Park?
It has an 'expansive and natural' sensory profile. It is very quiet ($45\text{dB}$) in the center, though noisier ($65\text{dB}$) near the perimeter roads. Visually, it is green and low-contrast. Physically, it is very stable ($5\text{Hz}$). It is the ultimate sensory refuge in Central London.
Can I access the Diana Memorial Fountain?
Yes, the fountain was designed specifically with accessibility in mind. It is a level-entry site with smooth stone paths. Wheelchair users can roll right up to the water’s edge.
How do I ask for the free shuttle?
Ask a park ranger or at the info point: 'Is the Liberty Drive buggy available?'. This is a free service specifically for those with mobility issues to help them cover the large distances in the park.

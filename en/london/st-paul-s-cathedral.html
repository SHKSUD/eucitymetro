Logistics Intelligence

Is St. Paul's Cathedral wheelchair accessible?
Yes, St. Paul's is fully accessible. While the West Front has historic steps, the South Churchyard entrance features a modern, high-capacity lift. The main Cathedral floor and the Crypt are 100% level ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration). However, the Stone and Golden Galleries (the Dome) are only accessible via hundreds of stairs ($500+$) and are not wheelchair accessible. The Whispering Gallery is also currently closed to all visitors.
Where are the accessible restrooms at St. Paul's?
Excellent PRM restrooms are located in the Crypt (Level -1), accessible via the main internal lift. They feature $1.8\text{-meter}$ turning radii and are maintained to a very high standard.
What is the best taxi drop-off for St. Paul's?
The best drop-off is at 'St. Paul's Churchyard' ($51.513100, -0.098100$) near the South Door. This allows for a very short ($15\text{-meter}$) roll on smooth pavement directly to the accessible lift entrance.
What is the sensory profile of St. Paul's?
It has a 'celestial and resonant' sensory profile. Acoustically, the reverb is legendary, reaching $75\text{dB}$ during choir or organ performances. Visually, the light under the dome is intense and uplifting. Physically, the floors are ultra-smooth ($0\text{Hz}$). For a sensory break, the Crypt is much quieter ($45\text{dB}$) and cooler.
Can I visit the Crypt in a wheelchair?
Yes, the Crypt is fully accessible via the same internal lift that connects the main floor. It is level throughout and houses the tombs of Nelson and Wellington, as well as the café and shop.
How do I ask for the accessible lift?
Look for staff in purple robes and ask: 'Where is the South Churchyard lift?' or 'I need step-free access to the Crypt.' They are exceptionally helpful and will often operate the lift for you.

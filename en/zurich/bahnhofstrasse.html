Logistics Intelligence

Is Bahnhofstrasse wheelchair accessible?
Yes, it is one of the most accessible luxury streets in the world. It is perfectly level, paved with smooth stone ($5\text{Hz}$ vibration), and all shops are required to provide step-free access or ramps. The tram stops are 'Curb-Level', allowing a 0Hz transition onto public transport.
Where are the accessible restrooms on Bahnhofstrasse?
The best PRM restrooms are in the department stores 'Jelmoli' and 'Manor' or the 'ZüriWC' at Paradeplatz (Eurokey required). These are 5-star facilities with $2.0\text{-meter}$ turning radii.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Bahnhofstrasse?
The best drop-off is 'Paradeplatz' ($47.369900, 8.538900$). It is a wide, flat plaza in the center of the shopping district with no curbs.
What is the sensory profile of Bahnhofstrasse?
It has a 'high-end and rhythmic' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is busy ($70\text{dB}$) but dominated by the hum of electric trams. Physically, it is ultra-smooth ($0\text{Hz}$). It is very bright in winter due to the famous 'Lucy' lights.
Can I enter the banks or chocolate shops?
Yes, most major establishments like 'Sprüngli' at Paradeplatz have level entries and wide automatic doors suitable for power-chairs.
How do I know which tram is accessible?
Look for the blue wheelchair symbol on the tram's digital display or the low-floor middle section. All trams on this route are now 95% accessible.

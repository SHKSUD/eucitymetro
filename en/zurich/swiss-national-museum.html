Logistics Intelligence

Is the National Museum accessible?
Yes, it is one of Switzerland's most modern accessible museums. It features seamless transitions between the historic 19th-century building and the new concrete extension. Lifts serve all levels, and there are no thresholds ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration). The park surrounding it (Platzspitz) is also 100% flat.
Where are the accessible restrooms at the Museum?
There are large, high-spec PRM restrooms in the foyer and on the lower levels of the new wing. They are very modern and follow the highest Swiss accessibility standards.
What is the best taxi drop-off for the National Museum?
The best drop-off is 'Museumstrasse' ($47.379100, 8.540200$), directly at the entrance. It is a dedicated drop-off zone with no curb.
What is the sensory profile of the Museum?
It has a 'hushed and architectural' sensory profile. The new wing is very quiet ($35\text{dB}$). Visually, the grey concrete is low-stimulus and calming. Physically, it is ultra-stable ($0\text{Hz}$).
Can I visit the museum cafe in a wheelchair?
Yes, the 'Spitz' restaurant and the museum bistro are both 100% level-access with plenty of space for wheelchairs between tables.
How do I get to the park?
The 'Platzspitz' park is located directly behind the museum. It is reached via a flat asphalt path (0Hz) from the museum's courtyard.

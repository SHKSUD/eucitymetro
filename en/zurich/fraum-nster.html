Logistics Intelligence

Is Fraumünster wheelchair accessible?
Yes, the main church floor is 100% accessible. While the building is historic, the side entrance has a flush threshold. The interior flooring is smooth stone ($0\text{Hz}$ vibration). The famous Chagall windows are easily viewable from a seated position. The Crypt museum is accessible via a platform lift—ask the staff for assistance.
Where are the accessible restrooms near Fraumünster?
There is a high-spec public 'ZüriWC' (accessible toilet) located on the Münsterhof square. It requires a Eurokey but is modern, self-cleaning, and has a $1.8\text{-meter}$ turning radius.
What is the best taxi drop-off for Fraumünster?
The best drop-off is 'Münsterhof' ($47.369700, 8.541300$). Taxis can pull right onto the square (level with the church). This is a 0Hz transition from the car to the church door.
What is the sensory profile of Fraumünster?
It has a 'hushed and colorful' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is very quiet ($40\text{dB}$), making it an excellent retreat. Visually, the blue and green light from the Chagall windows is intense but calming. Physically, it is ultra-stable ($0\text{Hz}$).
Can I visit the Crypt?
Yes. The Crypt was recently renovated and includes a platform lift. This allows wheelchair users to see the archaeological remains and the foundation of the original abbey without climbing stairs.
How do I ask for the crypt lift?
Ask at the welcome desk: 'Chönnted Sie mir bitte de Lift für d'Krypta zeige?' (Swiss German) or 'Could you show me the lift to the crypt?'. They will unlock the lift for you.

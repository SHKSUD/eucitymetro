Logistics Intelligence

Is Uetliberg accessible for wheelchairs?
Yes, but it is physically demanding. The train to the top is 100% accessible. From the station to the peak is a $10\text{-minute}$ roll on compacted gravel ($25\text{Hz}$ vibration) with a $10\%$ incline. Power-chairs handle this easily, but manual users will need high torque or assistance. The Hotel Uto Kulm at the top is fully accessible and has a large lift to the viewing terrace.
Where are the accessible restrooms at Uetliberg?
There is a high-quality PRM restroom inside the 'Hotel Uto Kulm' (accessible via the lobby lift). It is spacious, clean, and complies with Swiss SIA 500 standards ($2.0\text{-meter}$ turning radius).
What is the best taxi drop-off for Uetliberg?
Taxis cannot drive to the summit. The only way is the S10 train from Zurich HB. However, if you have a special permit, you can be dropped at 'Uetliberg Station' ($47.350300, 8.491400$), which is $600\text{m}$ from the summit.
What is the sensory profile of Uetliberg?
It has a 'natural and panoramic' sensory profile. Acoustically, it is quiet ($45\text{dB}$) with wind and bird sounds. Visually, it offers high-contrast, expansive views of the Alps and Lake Zurich. Physically, it is high-vibration ($25\text{Hz}$) due to the gravel.
How do I reach the very top viewing platform?
The Hotel Uto Kulm has a specialized large lift that goes to the summit terrace. You must enter the hotel and ask the staff to activate the lift for you—it is the only way to bypass the stairs to the 360-degree viewpoint.
How do I ask for the hotel lift in Swiss German?
Go to the reception and say: 'Grüezi, ich würdi gern ufd Ussichtsterrasse, chönnted Sie mir bitte de Lift zeige?'. They will escort you to the lift.
